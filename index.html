VisionBrush

Draw with your eyes. Guide it with your voice.

  1. LookFollow a few dots so it learns your eyes.
  2. SpeakSay “start” and “end” to draw. “fix” snaps shapes clean.
  3. CreateWhere you look is where the brush goes.
Click anywhere to beginor press Space

Voice commands need Chrome, Edge or Safari. In this browser VisionBrush runs hands-free: hold your gaze to place points and close your eyes for a second to start or stop.

Your camera video is processed on this computer and is never recorded or uploaded. Voice commands use your browser’s speech recognition. Works best on a laptop or desktop with the webcam at eye level and light on your face.

Open VisionBrush on a computer

It tracks your eyes with a webcam and turns your whole screen into the canvas, so it needs a laptop or desktop with Chrome or Edge.
